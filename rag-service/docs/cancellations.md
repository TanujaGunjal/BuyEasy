# Cancellations Policy

## Overview

You can cancel an order at any time before it has been delivered. Once an order reaches **Delivered** status, it can no longer be cancelled — you would need to request a return instead.

## When You Can Cancel

| Order Status | Can Cancel? |
|---|---|
| Pending | Yes — immediately |
| Processing | Yes — before dispatch |
| Shipped | Yes — but the parcel may already be in transit |
| Delivered | No — request a return instead |
| Cancelled | Already cancelled |

## How to Cancel an Order

1. Log in to BuyEasy and go to **My Orders**.
2. Select the order you want to cancel.
3. Click **Cancel Order** on the Order Details page.

Alternatively, ask the AI support agent: *"I want to cancel my order [order ID]."* The agent can check your order status and guide you through the process.

## Cancellations for Shipped Orders

If your order has already been dispatched, we will attempt to intercept the shipment. However, this is not always possible once a parcel is in transit. If the item arrives despite a cancellation request, please do not refuse delivery — instead, use our returns process to send it back.

## Refund for Cancelled Orders

If you paid by card or PayPal and your order is cancelled before shipment, your payment is refunded in full to your original payment method. Refund processing times are the same as for returns — typically 5–10 business days after cancellation is confirmed.

For Cash on Delivery orders that are cancelled before dispatch, no charge was ever made and no refund is necessary.

## Seller-Initiated Cancellations

In rare cases, BuyEasy may cancel your order if:
- The item goes out of stock after your order is placed
- A pricing error is identified
- We are unable to verify your payment

In these situations, you will be notified by email and a full refund will be issued automatically.
