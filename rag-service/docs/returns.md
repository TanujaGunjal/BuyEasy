# Returns Policy

## Overview

BuyEasy accepts returns on most items within 30 days of delivery. This page explains who is eligible, what items qualify, and how to start the process through our AI support agent.

## Who Can Return an Item

Any customer whose order status shows **Delivered** and whose delivery was confirmed within the last 30 calendar days may request a return. The 30-day window is measured from the date your order was marked delivered in our system — not from the date you place the order or the date you received the shipping notification.

If your delivery date is not recorded in our system (this can occasionally happen with some carriers), your return request will require manual review by our support team rather than being automatically approved.

## Items That Cannot Be Returned

The following categories are **non-returnable** under any circumstances:

- **Food items** — perishable goods, edible products, supplements, and consumables cannot be returned for hygiene and safety reasons. This applies regardless of whether the item is sealed or unopened.

All other product categories — Electronics, Clothing, Sports, Home & Furniture, Home & Garden, Accessories, Books, Hand Bags, and Other — are eligible for return provided the 30-day window has not expired.

## How to Start a Return

1. Log in to BuyEasy and open the **AI Support Chat** (the chat bubble at the bottom-right of every page).
2. Tell the agent you would like to return an order and provide your order ID, or mention your recent order if you have already discussed it in the same chat session.
3. The agent will check your eligibility automatically using our policy engine. If your order is eligible, the agent will submit a refund request for admin review.
4. You do **not** need to ship the item back before the refund is submitted. Our team will contact you with return shipping instructions if required.

## Return Window Details

| Situation | Outcome |
|---|---|
| Delivered, within 30 days, non-Food item | Eligible for return |
| Delivered, within 30 days, Food item | Not eligible (non-returnable category) |
| Delivered, more than 30 days ago | Not eligible (return window expired) |
| Not yet delivered (Pending / Processing / Shipped) | Not eligible (must be delivered first) |
| Delivered status set but delivery date missing | Manual review required |

## After You Submit a Return Request

Your request is reviewed by a BuyEasy admin. You will receive a notification once a decision has been made. If approved, the refund is issued to your original payment method. See our Refunds Policy for timeline details.
