# Shipping Policy

## Overview

BuyEasy ships to addresses within India and select international destinations. All shipping costs and estimated delivery times are shown during checkout before you confirm your order.

## Shipping Timeframes

| Shipping Method | Estimated Delivery |
|---|---|
| Standard Shipping | 5–7 business days |
| Express Shipping | 2–3 business days |
| Same-Day (select cities) | Ordered before 12:00 PM local time |

Delivery estimates begin from the date your order is dispatched, not the date you place the order. Orders placed on weekends or public holidays are typically dispatched on the next business day.

## Order Processing Time

Most orders are processed and dispatched within 1–2 business days of payment confirmation. During peak periods (sales events, holidays), processing may take up to 3 business days.

## Tracking Your Order

Once your order is dispatched, you will receive a tracking number by email. You can also ask the BuyEasy AI support agent: *"Where is my order?"* or *"Track my delivery for order [order ID]."*

Tracking information is updated by our carrier partners and may not reflect real-time movement. Allow up to 24 hours after dispatch for tracking to activate.

## Shipping Costs

Shipping charges are calculated at checkout based on your delivery location, the total weight of your order, and the selected shipping method. Orders above a certain value may qualify for free standard shipping — check the checkout page for the current threshold.

## Failed Deliveries

If a delivery attempt fails (no one available to receive, incorrect address, access issues), the carrier will typically make up to two more attempts. After three failed attempts, the package may be returned to our warehouse. Contact our support team if you need to arrange redelivery.

## Shipping to Multiple Addresses

At this time, each order can only be shipped to a single address. If you need items delivered to multiple addresses, please place separate orders.
