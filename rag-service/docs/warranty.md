# Warranty Policy

## Overview

BuyEasy acts as a marketplace and retailer for products from multiple brands and manufacturers. Warranty coverage depends on the specific product and its manufacturer. This page explains how to find warranty information and what support is available through BuyEasy.

## Manufacturer Warranties

Most Electronics, Appliances, and Sports equipment sold on BuyEasy come with a **manufacturer warranty**. The warranty period and terms are set by the manufacturer, not by BuyEasy. Typical coverage periods:

| Category | Typical Warranty |
|---|---|
| Electronics (laptops, phones) | 1 year manufacturer warranty |
| Home appliances | 1–2 year manufacturer warranty |
| Sports equipment | 6 months to 1 year |
| Clothing and accessories | No manufacturer warranty (covered by our returns policy) |
| Books | No warranty |
| Food items | No warranty (non-returnable) |

## How to Make a Warranty Claim

Warranty claims are handled directly by the manufacturer or their authorised service centres. BuyEasy does not process warranty repairs or replacements, but we can help you:

1. Identify the manufacturer's support contact from your product details page.
2. Provide proof of purchase (your BuyEasy order ID and payment confirmation email are accepted by most manufacturers).

Contact the manufacturer's support line with:
- Your product model and serial number
- Your BuyEasy order ID and date of purchase
- A description of the defect

## BuyEasy's Own Guarantee

Separate from manufacturer warranties, if a product arrives **damaged or defective** and you report it within **30 days of delivery**, you may be eligible for a return and refund under our Returns Policy — regardless of the manufacturer warranty status.

- Use the AI Support Chat to report a damaged or defective item within 30 days of delivery.
- The agent will check your return eligibility and submit a refund request for admin review if you qualify.
- Food items are excluded even if damaged on arrival, as they are non-returnable.

## What Is Not Covered

BuyEasy's return and refund policy does not cover:

- Damage caused by misuse, accidental damage, or normal wear and tear
- Items returned after the 30-day return window
- Food items (non-returnable regardless of condition)
- Items where the delivery date is not confirmed in our system

For issues outside this scope, please contact the manufacturer directly using your warranty documentation.
